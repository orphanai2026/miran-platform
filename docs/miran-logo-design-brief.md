# برومت تصميم شامل — شعار مِران

## المفهوم العام

شعار نصي هجين (Hybrid Logotype) لمنصة تعليم الناي الشرقي "مِران". أول حرفين من اسم الكلمة ("م" و"ر") يُكتبان بخط عربي حديث غامق عادي، بينما آخر حرفين ("ا" و"ن") يتحوّلان إلى رمز تشكيلي متصل بصريًا بالنص، بحيث تُقرأ الكلمة كاملة "مِران" رغم أن نصفها الأخير رمزي لا حرفي.

الهوية العامة: روحانية-تأملية، متجذرة في التراث الموسيقي الشرقي (آلة الناي)، بسيطة وحديثة لا مزخرفة، بلا أي شخصية أو صورة بشرية.

## البرومت الكامل (لأي أداة توليد صور أو تصميم)

```
Professional flat vector logo design for a music education platform called "مران" (Miran), an Arabic ney flute training app.

COMPOSITION: A hybrid Arabic logotype where the first two letters of the word
"مران" are rendered as normal bold typography on the right side, and the last
two letters (Alif and Nun) are transformed into a connected decorative icon
positioned to the left of the text, flowing as one continuous visual unit in
right-to-left reading order.

TEXT PART ("مر"): Bold, geometric, modern Arabic sans-serif typeface, style
similar to Cairo ExtraBold (weight 800) or Alexandria Bold. Solid dark ink
color #1C1C1A. Clean, crisp, perfectly legible Arabic letterforms with
correct letter connections, professional branding quality — this is real
readable Arabic text, not decorative shapes.

ICON PART — represents Alif + Nun, positioned left of and connected to the text:
- Alif: a vertical zigzag line, faceted angular waveform like a lightning
  bolt or sound waveform, deep teal green color #0F6E56, thick rounded
  stroke (~16px weight at this scale), rounded line caps and joins.
- Along the zigzag: six small circles alternating between solid filled dark
  circles (#1C1C1A) and open ring-outline circles, evenly spaced along the
  path, representing musical finger holes (open/closed fingering).
- Nun: at the bottom, the zigzag flows smoothly into an open circular
  hook/loop shape curling like the numeral 6, golden amber color #BA7517,
  same stroke weight as the zigzag, with a visible open gap on its upper
  right side.
- Accent dot: at the junction where the zigzag meets the golden loop, one
  distinctive dark circular dot (#1C1C1A) with a small light cream
  highlight circle inside it (#FAF6EC), like a stylized eye or a musical
  note head catching light. This is the single accent focal point of the mark.

COLOR PALETTE (exact hex values, use only these):
- Teal (Alif / primary): #0F6E56
- Gold/amber (Nun / accent): #BA7517
- Ink (text / dots / dark accents): #1C1C1A
- Cream (background / light highlight): #FAF6EC

BACKGROUND: solid cream off-white #FAF6EC, no texture, no pattern, no gradient background.

STYLE: flat design, no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D bevels, no glow or
neon effects, no photographic textures, no stock-icon look, no mascots, no
human figures, no religious iconography beyond the abstract halo/circle
motif already described. Clean geometric vector illustration, professional
logo/branding quality, centered composition with generous negative space
margin on all sides, square aspect ratio 1:1.

MOOD: spiritual, contemplative, warm, rooted in Middle Eastern musical
heritage, modern and minimal rather than ornate or busy. Should feel
completely original — do not reference, copy, or resemble any existing
brand, company logo, or trademarked symbol.

MUST WORK AT SMALL SIZE: the mark must remain legible and recognizable when
scaled down to a 20x20px icon size (browser favicon scale), even in a
single flat color with no gradients.

MUST WORK ON DARK BACKGROUNDS: also generate or adapt a reversed/knockout
version — teal becomes cream #FAF6EC, gold becomes a lighter amber
#EFB35A, ink text becomes cream #FAF6EC, on a dark teal-black background
#14312A.

DO NOT: add any additional text beyond "مر", do not add taglines or
slogans, do not add extra decorative flourishes beyond what is described,
do not distort or misspell the Arabic letterforms, do not add a border or
frame around the whole logo, do not add any character, mascot, or human
face anywhere in the design.
```
